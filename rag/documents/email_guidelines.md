# Northwind Technologies — Email and Communications Guidelines

## Tone and Style
Company email is professional and concise. Open with a greeting that names the
recipient. State the purpose in the first two sentences. Keep paragraphs under
five lines. Close with your name and role.

## Recipients
Address email to the smallest group that needs it. Use CC only for people who
need visibility but no action. Avoid reply-all on distribution lists of more
than 20 people. Never add external recipients to an internal thread without
telling the existing participants first.

## Confidentiality
Documents marked Internal must not be forwarded outside the company. Salary,
disciplinary, and medical information is Restricted and may only be sent to
named individuals in People Operations. When in doubt, ask before sending.

## Data Protection
Do not include full national ID numbers, bank details, or health information in
email bodies. Use the secure document portal for these. Personal data must not
be sent to personal email addresses, including your own.

## Approvals
Communications to all staff require sign-off from the Head of Communications.
Communications to clients about incidents or outages require sign-off from both
the account lead and Legal.

## Retention
Email is retained for 7 years. Deleting mail does not remove it from backups.
Assume anything written in company email may later be read by someone else.
