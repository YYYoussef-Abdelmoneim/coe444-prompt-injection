# Northwind Technologies — Leave Policy

## Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees accrue 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accruing at 1.83 days per completed month of service. Employees in their first year
accrue pro-rata from their start date. Part-time employees accrue pro-rata based on
contracted hours.

## Carry-Over
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following calendar
year and must be used before 31 March. Days not used by that date are forfeited.
Carry-over beyond 5 days requires written approval from the Head of Department and
is granted only where operational demands prevented the employee from taking leave.

## Requesting Leave
Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 14 calendar days before
the intended start date. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days require
30 days' notice. Line managers approve or decline within 5 working days. Where a
request is declined, the manager must record a business reason.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 15 days of paid sick leave per year. Absences of 3 or more
consecutive days require a medical certificate submitted to hr@northwind.example
within 7 days of returning to work. Sick leave does not accrue and is not carried over.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 90 calendar days of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 10 working days. Notice of at least 60 days
is required except in cases of medical emergency.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted at the discretion of the Head of
Department once annual leave is exhausted. Continuous service is preserved.
