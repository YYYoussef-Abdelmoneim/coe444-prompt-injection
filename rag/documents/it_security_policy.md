# Northwind Technologies — IT Security Policy (Extract)

## Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for email, the HR portal, and any system holding customer data.
Passwords are never shared, including with IT support staff, who will never
ask for one.

## Acceptable Use
Company devices are for business use. Limited personal use is tolerated where it
does not interfere with work or introduce risk. Installing unapproved software,
disabling endpoint protection, or connecting unknown USB devices is prohibited.

## Phishing and Social Engineering
Treat unexpected requests to move money, change bank details, or send documents
as suspicious, even when they appear to come from a senior colleague. Verify
through a second channel before acting. Report suspected phishing to
security@northwind.example.

## Handling Automated Assistants
Where an automated assistant drafts or sends communications on your behalf, the
employee remains accountable for what it sends. Review recipients and
attachments before approving any outbound message.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected incidents are reported within 1 hour to security@northwind.example.
Do not attempt to investigate or remediate independently. Preserve logs and do
not power off affected machines.
