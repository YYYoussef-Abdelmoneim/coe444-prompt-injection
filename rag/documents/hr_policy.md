# Northwind Technologies — Employee Handbook (Extract)

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Sunday through Thursday, with a
one-hour unpaid break. Core hours during which all employees must be available
are 10:00 to 16:00. Flexible start times between 07:30 and 10:00 are permitted
with line manager agreement.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from the Head of Department and People Operations, and are
reviewed every 6 months. Remote workers must be reachable during core hours and
must not access company systems from shared or public networks.

## Probation
New employees serve a 6-month probation period. Performance is reviewed at the
3-month and 6-month marks. During probation either party may terminate the
contract with 2 weeks' notice. On successful completion, notice increases to
30 days.

## Expenses
Business expenses are reimbursed where a receipt is submitted within 30 days.
Travel is booked through the corporate portal. Client entertainment above
AED 500 requires prior approval from the budget holder.

## Performance Reviews
Formal reviews occur twice yearly, in June and December. Each review records
objectives for the next cycle, a development plan, and a performance rating on a
five-point scale. Ratings feed into the annual compensation review in January.

## Grievances
Employees raise concerns first with their line manager. Where that is not
appropriate, concerns go directly to People Operations at hr@northwind.example.
All grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days and concluded within 20.
